Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dr. Sabyasachi Roy ("I", "me", "my") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit sabyroy.com (the "Site").

1. Information I Collect

I collect the following types of information:

  • Email address and name — when you subscribe to the newsletter or use a contact form.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, referral source, via Google Analytics (if you consent to cookies).
  • Cookies — small text files stored in your browser to remember your preferences.

2. How I Use Your Information

  • To send you the newsletter and resources you subscribed to.
  • To understand how visitors use the Site and improve it.
  • To remember your consent preferences.

I do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required to operate the Site (e.g., email service provider Kit/ConvertKit).

3. Email Marketing

If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email is stored with Kit (ConvertKit), my email service provider. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email. Kit's privacy policy is available at kit.com/privacy.

4. Cookies

This Site uses the following cookies:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) — only set if you accept cookies. These help me understand Site usage anonymously.
  • Functional cookies — stored in your browser's localStorage to remember your preferences (e.g., cookie consent, subscriber login session). These do not track you across sites.

You can decline analytics cookies via the cookie banner when you first visit the Site.

5. Your Rights (UK/GDPR)

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to: access the personal data I hold about you; request correction or deletion; withdraw consent at any time; lodge a complaint with the ICO (ico.org.uk).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact me at the email address in my newsletter.

6. Data Retention

Email subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics default settings (26 months).

7. Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.
